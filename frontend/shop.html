Hardware Return Process

How customer returns are handled

Customer side

  • Log in and open My Account → My Orders.
  • Select the delivered order and use Request Return.
  • Choose the product, quantity, reason, condition and upload issue photos.
  • Admin reviews the request and updates the next action from the dashboard.

Conditions

  • Wrong item / missing parts / visible transport damage: within 48 hours.
  • Defective or quality issue: within 7 days.
  • Unused standard-stock items: within 14 days if sealed or unused.
  • Custom, cut-to-size, mixed, tinted and special-order items are not accepted for change-of-mind returns.
